Work with the DELA Lab

The DELA Lab welcomes Clemson students interested in how AI and other emerging technologies can support teaching and learning.

Admitted and current students Prospective graduate students

For admitted and current Clemson students

Ph.D. students

I welcome Ph.D. students in education and computing who are interested in collaborating on DELA Lab research. If you already have a Ph.D. advisor and I’m not your advisor, please talk with them before reaching out so you can discuss how working with the lab might fit into your doctoral research.

Master’s students

If you’d like to contribute to an ongoing research project, I’d be glad to hear about the skills and perspectives you’d bring to the lab. As a lab member, you’ll have opportunities to collaborate on research, share work in progress, get feedback from other lab members, and contribute to publications where appropriate.

Undergraduate students

If you’re studying education or computing and want to get involved in research, I encourage you to reach out. Working alongside lab members is a chance to see how a research project takes shape and to contribute to work that connects computing and emerging technologies with education.

How to get involved

I keep the lab small enough to give each member meaningful mentorship and feedback. If you’re interested in working with me, send me a brief email with your Clemson program and degree level, whether you’ve been admitted or are already enrolled, and the lab project or research topic that interests you. Feel free to include any questions you have.

Email me at iadisa@clemson.edu

When funding is available, the lab may offer paid hourly positions for project development or other research activities. Openings will be posted on the lab website, and current lab members will receive priority.

For prospective graduate students

Learning Sciences M.Ed.

Clemson’s Learning Sciences M.Ed. is fully online. If you’re interested in working with me during your master’s program, please reach out after you’ve been admitted. I don’t participate in reviewing master’s applications.

Explore the Learning Sciences M.Ed.

Learning Sciences Ph.D.

Clemson offers the Learning Sciences Ph.D. in a HyFlex format, with options to attend classes in person or join live online. The Learning Sciences faculty review applications together, and I typically don’t meet with prospective students before they submit an application.

If you’re interested in working with me, you’re welcome to mention that in your letter of intent. Briefly describe a research question or topic you’d like to explore and how it connects with my research or a project in the lab.

Clemson lists January 15 as the deadline for all Ph.D. application materials. Review the admission requirements and select Apply Now on the program page to start your application.

If you have questions about the program, funding, or assistantships after reviewing the program page, contact Madison Hudson.
