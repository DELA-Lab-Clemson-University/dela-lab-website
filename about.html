About DELA

Learning at the intersection of people, design, and technology.

DELA Lab studies the design and integration of emerging technologies for learning—both as tools for learning and as new domains of knowledge.

Our approach

Through codesign with educators, students, researchers, and technology designers, we ground innovation in the realities of K–12 classrooms.

Our values

Human-centered · Collaborative · Curious

We study authentic educational contexts, design with the people who use technology, and turn evidence into better learning possibilities.
